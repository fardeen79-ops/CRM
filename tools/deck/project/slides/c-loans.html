Control 4 · Loan details

Buy-outs and auto loans cannot be submitted half-described

Personal loans

Fresh, top-up or buy-out, with tenure capped at 48 months. A buy-out must name the personal loan being bought, its bank and amount. Every loan confirms whether there are secondary buy-outs: cards with limits and banks, non-STL loans, auto loans or mortgages.

Auto loans

New or used, car make, model and year, dealer, lead source, rate of interest and tenure up to 60 months.

Why it matters

The bank's liability picture is complete on day one, and the reports can show which banks we are buying out and where auto loan leads come from.
