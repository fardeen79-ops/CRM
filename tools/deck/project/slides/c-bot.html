A verification bot that calls the customer, taught and approved by our own people

A bot that makes the verification call

It phones the customer, checks it is speaking to them, and asks one question per detail: name, product, employer, salary. Every line it says is taught on one page, and a practice panel plays the call before anything is saved.

Claude behind it, within limits

The AI reads only the answers the rules cannot settle, such as "twenty-five K" or "the national oil company", and can word the conversation naturally. It never reads a customer's details back, and it never rejects a file.

People stay in charge

The verification team leader asks for each call; governance and a business head both approve; calls go out only between 09:00 and 18:00. By default the bot decides nothing and a processor reviews the result; the business can allow it to complete a fully confirmed verification.
