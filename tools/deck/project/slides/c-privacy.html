Control 6 · Customer data

Once a file is submitted, sales staff cannot take the customer's data with them

Hidden and masked

The sales person sees the customer's phone numbers as Hidden and the salary only as a mask, with no way to reveal them. Processors and managers reveal only what they need, and each reveal is logged. Emirates ID and passport numbers are not kept at all.

Watermarked

Every screen carries a faint watermark of the signed-in user and the time, so a photo or screenshot can be traced to its source. Uploaded documents are stamped the same way.

Why it matters

The bank's customers' data stays inside the file. A leaver takes no phone list, and the governance team can answer "who looked at this customer" from the log.
