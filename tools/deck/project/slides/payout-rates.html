Where it shows

On each file, with the working. On the dashboard as payout earned this cycle and payout in the open pipeline. In the sourcing report per staff member, in the case register per file, and in the down-sell report as payout lost.

Who sees it

Only the business head and the Dubai MIS team. Sales staff, processors, team leaders, sales managers, ASMs and governance never see what a file earns, and the rates themselves are visible and changeable only to those two.
