Verification · Do Not Call Register

A customer we cannot call gets a ready-made email asking for permission

The file stops, the email is drafted

When the processor marks a customer as being on the UAE Do Not Call Register, the file waits and the CRM drafts the email itself: the customer's name, the Emirates NBD product they applied for, and the exact reply that gives permission.

Sent by the right person

Only the processing team or the team leader see the draft, signed in their own name. Copy it or open it in the mailbox in one click. The sales person is never the one contacting a customer on the register.

Why it matters

No call is made without consent, the wording is the same every time, and the file carries the reference so the customer's reply can be logged and the file sent back for verification.
