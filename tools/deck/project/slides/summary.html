In brief

One system, three outcomes for the business

Control at the point of sale

The bank's product rules are checked while the sales person is still with the customer: the right card for the salary, complete loan details, a manager's approval for exceptions. The tabs the bank issues are on a register of their own, and the business defines who sees what.

Revenue loss made visible

Every card sold below what the customer qualified for is counted in payout lost, by file, by staff member and by team, in the same cycle it happens, visible to the business head and Dubai MIS only.

Reports management can run

Twenty-two reports on sourcing, loans, pipeline, verification, targets, cards, exceptions, incentives for every scheme, profit and loss and access, for any cycle or date range, by region, on screen or as a spreadsheet.

Underneath it all: customer data is masked, watermarked and logged, each role sees only its own region or team, and the whole system runs on our own server with no outside services.
