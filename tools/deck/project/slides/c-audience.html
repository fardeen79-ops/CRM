Control 7 · Who sees what

Each role sees its own slice, and every file says who can read it

RoleFiles visible
Sales staffTheir own files only
ProcessingTheir own region, Dubai or Abu Dhabi
Team leader, ASM, sales managerFiles of staff in their own team
MIS, business head, governanceEverything, with a region switch

When a staff member changes team, open files move with them for the cycle; completed files stay credited to the old team. The Discussion on every file lists exactly who can read and post, so nothing is said to an audience nobody expected.
