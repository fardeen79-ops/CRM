Control 9 · Roles

Access is defined by the business, not hard-wired: a role gets only what it is given

Nine built-in roles

Sales, processing, team leader, ASM, sales manager, MIS, business head, governance and IT, each with its own screens, reports and uploads. These cannot be widened.

Roles the agency defines

IT, Dubai MIS and business heads build a role on a built-in one and tick what it gets: which screens, which reports, which uploads, whether it can download, whether it sees pricing. Never more than its base.

Why it matters

An auditor, an analyst or a bank visitor can be given exactly the view they need, and nothing else, without a code change. Every refusal is enforced on the server, not just hidden on screen.
