The name

The system has a name of its own: STRIDE. Sales in stride.

STRIDE

Sales in stride.

A one-word name signals a product the team built, not a tool it was given. A stride is forward motion, rhythm and a bigger step than a walk: the step-change from spreadsheets and MIS cuts to one system. The mark's three chevrons are the pipeline read left to right; the amber one is the step that lands.

SSales · leads, pipeline and conversion

TTracking · every file, follow-up and call

RReporting · dashboards, MIS cuts, P&L

IIncentives · payouts against the schemes

DData · one source of truth for staff and products

EEngagement · nudges, boosters, the customer journey

In the CRM · the foot of every screen

Navy and amber, deliberately not the bank's colours: STRIDE sits alongside any partner's material without imitating it. The brand appears only here, small and quiet, on every screen in light and dark mode.
