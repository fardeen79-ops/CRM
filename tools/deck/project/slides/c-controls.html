Verification · Controls

Files that need a second look wait for the right person

Approval before verification

A file sourced on a Sunday or entered after 6 pm is flagged and waits for the team leader or sales manager. A card sold below its salary requirement waits for the same decision. Nothing reaches a processor until the team has said yes.

The right processor, per product

The verification team leader allocates processors to each sales team leader by product: cards to one, loans to another, or all to one. A routed file reaches only its processor's queue.

Honest outcomes

A processor who cannot reach the customer can still complete on a deviation, with a mandatory reason on the file. Governance marks each complaint valid or invalid, and a valid complaint removes the file from the sales person's incentive.
