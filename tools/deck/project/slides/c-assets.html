Control 8 · Tab register

Every sourcing tab the bank issues is accounted for, by an IT role that sees nothing else

What is recorded

Per tab: serial and tab numbers, the charger, stylus and card reader issued with it, Etisalat or du SIM and number, the Microsoft Entra ID and the registered mobile. One tab per sales person, shown on their own dashboard.

Custody at all times

Active in use, with IT custody, handed over on exit, or returned to the bank on a dated record. Every move is logged with who did it. Tabs can be loaded in bulk from a sheet.

Why it matters

The IT department keeps the register from its own account, with no access to customer files. An inventory report can be pulled at any time for the bank or an audit, with the leavers' tabs and spares counted.
