Control 2 · Card eligibility

The right card for the salary, checked while the customer is present

Category and points fill in

Choosing a card from the bank's list of 34 fills its category (Mass, Premium, Super Premium), the salary it needs and the points it earns. Nobody types these.

Salary qualifies for more

If the salary allows a higher category, the form lists those cards and the sales person can switch with one tap. Selling lower is still allowed, but it is recorded as a down-sell.

Salary below the card's minimum

A warning blocks the file unless a reason is given: product deviation or new promotion. The reason is stamped on the file and reported.
