Management · Personal loan yield

Every loan book is priced against the 8.49% minimum, from each sales person up to the region

What yield means here

The average interest rate on the personal loans disbursed in the cycle, weighted by amount, so a large loan at a low rate pulls the figure down as it should. Only disbursed loans count, at the amount disbursed and the rate on the file.

One minimum, every level

8.49% is the floor. Each sales person sees their own yield; a team leader sees the team and each member; managers see their team leaders; the business head sees the regions. Green at or above the minimum, red below, with the gap in points and the split by fresh, top-up and buy-out.

Why it matters

Volume alone can hide a book sold too cheap. The buy-out share shows where the rate is coming from, and a report with every level, by region, goes to a spreadsheet for the bank's review.
