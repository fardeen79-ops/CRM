Management · Daily discipline

Every leader sees who is on zero and which days the team went quiet

Staff on zero

How many of the team, as a count and a share, have no end or disbursal this cycle and no submission at all, split by product and down the hierarchy, with the names highlighted and linked to their files.

The submission calendar

One cell per working day of the cycle. For a sales person, green when they submitted and red when not. For a leader, green when 70% or more of the team submitted, orange from 50%, red below. Sunday is the day off.

Why it matters

A quiet week shows the same day, not at month end. The team leader, the sales manager and the business head all read the same cells, so the conversation about a red patch starts early.
