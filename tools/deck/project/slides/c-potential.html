Sales · The push

Each sales person sees what their open files could earn, and what the next step is worth

What is in the system

The scheme block shows how many files are still open, what they would pay if they all completed this cycle, and how much more than today that is.

How far to the money

Then the gap: the points or production still needed to start earning, from today and after those files complete, and for loan staff the next band and the card threshold.

In dirhams, not percentages

"Reach AED 600,000 and the whole production pays 0.40%, that is AED 2,400 in hand, and every extra AED 100,000 adds AED 400." The push is written for the person, in money.
