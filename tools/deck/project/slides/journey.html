The journey of a file

Four stages, with a control at each hand-off

1 · Sales

Source the file

Customer, products and loan details captured, region and salary bank recorded; a lead sheet can be scanned to start the file.

2 · Rules

Checked at once

Card eligibility against salary, mandatory loan details, and a manager's approval when a rule is bent.

3 · Processing

Verified by call

A processor confirms every detail with the customer, logs call-backs and sends corrections back to sales.

4 · Management

Measured

Completion, points earned, points lost, deviations and turnaround roll up to team, region and business.

Eight roles

Sales, processing, team leader, ASM, sales manager, MIS, business head and governance, each with its own screens.

Four products, two regions

Credit cards, personal loans, auto loans and accounts, sourced in Dubai and Abu Dhabi.

Built for the sales floor

Works in a phone browser: scan, dictate and submit from the customer's desk, no app to install.
