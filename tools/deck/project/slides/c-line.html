Verification · On our own line

The bot can call from our own SIP line, with nothing leaving our server

No telephony provider in the call

A second calling service dials over the company's own SIP line from a server we control. The customer sees our number; no cloud telephony company carries the call or sees the customer's details.

Speech and recordings stay on the server

The bot's voice and the recognition of what the customer says are open-source programs installed on that server. Recordings are files in a folder there, played inside the CRM with a signed request. Nobody outside hears or stores a call.

Same bot, same controls

The CRM cannot tell the two services apart: the same requests, two approvals, calling hours, taught lines, results and transcripts. The AI is off unless we switch it on, so by default the whole call stays in-house. A one-command check tests the line and places a test call before go-live.

$ node bot/sip-check.js --call 050 123 4567

STRIDE · 15

The point for the CEO: the verification bot does not need Twilio or any cloud voice service. With our own SIP line and two open-source speech programs on our server, no third party hears, stores or transcribes a customer call. The check output shown is illustrative of what IT runs before go-live. What IT needs: the SIP line details from the provider, the two speech programs installed, and the audio ports opened towards the provider. Compliance still signs off the caller number and the recording notice.
