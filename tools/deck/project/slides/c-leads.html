Before the file · Leads

Every prospect is on record before it is a file, and nothing is retyped when it becomes one

A sales person's own pipeline

Name, mobile, company, salary, the product of interest, source and a follow-up date. Seen only by the sales person and their team leader; nobody else can open it.

Three ways a lead ends

Converted into a case, with every detail carried into the sourcing form and the lead linked to the file. Or marked not interested, or not eligible, with the reason kept.

Why it matters

Leaders see how much is in the pipeline and what is due today, not just what has been filed. Lost prospects are counted, with reasons, instead of disappearing from a notebook.
