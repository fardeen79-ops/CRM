Sales · Boosters

A product push with dates, shown to the people it is for, with progress as it runs

Run a campaign in minutes

The business head or MIS names the booster, picks the product, the dates and the reward, and who it is for: both regions or one, the product's core staff or everyone, or only some teams. It can be edited, paused or deleted at any time.

Seen by exactly the right people

It appears on the dashboard of every sales person it covers and of their team leaders and managers, two weeks before it starts and while it runs, with the dates, the days left and the reward in plain words.

Progress while it runs

Each card counts the files completed in the window with that product, the dirhams disbursed for loans, and for leaders how many of the covered staff have contributed and who is covered. Files with a valid complaint do not count.
