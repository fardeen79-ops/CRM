Targets and staff

Targets come from the staff master and achievement from completed files

One record per staff member

HRMS code as the login, region, team leader, ASM and manager, dates of joining and leaving, salary and core product. Only MIS and the business head create or change accounts; leavers are switched off on their leaving date.

Targets by salary band

Card and auto loan targets in points, personal loan targets in disbursal value, generated for the cycle from the staff member's salary band. Achievement counts each completed file at its real points.
